================================================================================
ALERT LEVELS
================================================================================

🟢 GREEN (< 0.78")
   No significant flood risk
   Action: Continue normal operations

🟡 ADVISORY (0.78" - 1.24")
   Elevated rainfall expected
   Action: Monitor conditions, prepare resources

🟠 WATCH (1.24" - 2.02")
   Significant flood risk
   Action: Activate emergency operations center

🔴 WARNING (> 2.02")
   Imminent flood threat
   Action: Issue public warnings, evacuate if needed
